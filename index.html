Industrial Cold Rooms Control Panel

Electrical controls designed with AutoCAD Electrical

Designed and implemented industrial cold rooms electrical control systems. The system includes multiple protection devices including phase failure protection to protect the compressor from running in unintended direction. Also includes multiple warning/failure indicators to enhance fast troubleshooting.
